## Abstract

When the CGM stage thresholds are placed as Einstein speeds in the open unit ball, every closed walk among those points carries a residual spatial rotation. This document is a metrological audit of those rotations. It classifies each reported number by origin layer, transport law, invariant type, and physical status, and it answers three questions. Which angles are forced by the thresholds under Fermi–Walker / geodesic transport? Which depend on a laboratory boost-composition protocol? Which are coordinate artifacts of a singular chart?

The canonical connection is unique and closed. Six independent constructions recover the BU dual-pole loop angle δ_BU = 4 · arctan(k(π/4) · k(m_a)) to the working floor, including a regular Cartesian path-ordered exponential of the Palge–Pfeifer Thomas 1-form that Analysis_Holonomy.md left open. On all 360 closed walks of length at most 5, origin-gyration equals geodesic holonomy. The canonical spectrum contains exactly six distinct angle bins, generated by three mutually orthogonal origin-pair Wigner rotations together with the identity on the collinear BU pole pair.

The laboratory connection is distinct and generically open. Only the four collinear BU± out-backs close in a single inertial frame. The spherical-chart path-ordered exponential is a singular readout of the same mass-shell connection, not a third holonomy. Gravity consumes only {m_a, δ_BU, ρ, Δ} from this geometry. Named couplings of the remaining canonical bins, of the compact SU(2) commutator, and of the compact–hyperbolic residual are unknown.

## 1. Scope

Analysis_Holonomy.md establishes that closed paths leave a residual transformation in the CGM state geometry. It derives the closed form of δ_BU, proves that the palindrome preserves that angle while transporting its axis, and identifies the same angle with the origin-gyr word, the Ungar dual-pole defect, and mass-shell geodesic holonomy. A rate Ω = θ / T needs a lab-time parameterization of acceleration, or a cycle duration assigned by the gravity analysis. Neither clock is computed here.

Two mathematical identifications are kept distinct. The four stage priors u_p, o_p, m_a, and θ_CS are logical numbers. Placing the first three as Einstein-ball speeds β is an extra realization step. Rapidities η = atanh(β) and Poincaré radii k(β) = β / (1 + √(1 − β²)) are alternative radial coordinates on the same manifold, not additional constants. Physical applications of the quantities classified here, including the fine-structure constant and the gravitational coupling, are treated in Analysis_Fine_Structure.md and Analysis_Gravity.md. This run does not recompute τ_G, g₁, or Mercury.

The following vocabulary is used throughout.

A **connection** is a rule that assigns a spatial rotation to a path in velocity space. The **canonical** connection is Fermi–Walker / geodesic transport on the forward mass shell, equivalently origin-gyration in the Einstein gyrogroup. The **lab** connection is the rotational part of a product of successive Lorentz boosts written in one inertial frame. The **chart** connection is the Palge–Pfeifer spin connection evaluated either in Cartesian velocity coordinates or in a spherical chart. A **holonomy** is the residual rotation of a closed path under a stated connection. A **protocol residual** is the difference between two connections on the same vertices. A **coordinate readout** is an evaluation of one connection in a chart that is singular on the path.

## 3. Three Transport Connections

Three rules act on the same vertices.

**Canonical (∇^can).** Between successive four-velocities q_i on the forward unit hyperboloid, take the unique rotation-free Lorentz boost (transvection) T_i with T_i q_i = q_{i+1}. The path product conjugated into the rest frame of the basepoint is an SO(3) holonomy. Equivalently, compose origin-based gyrations along the Einstein differences, or evaluate the Ungar gyrotriangle defect on the visited vertices. Palge and Pfeifer identify this rotation with holonomy of the Levi-Civita / spin connection on the mass shell [4]. Out-and-back paths invert and cancel. Refactorizations of a closed loop that preserve the vertex set leave the conjugacy angle invariant.

**Lab (∇^lab).** For successive stage velocities p_i write d_i = ⊖p_i ⊕ p_{i+1} and L(d_i) for the corresponding boost in one inertial frame. Factor the word L(d_0) ⋯ L(d_{n−1}) as a net boost B(u_final) times a spatial rotation R. The status is CLOSED_LAB_PRECESSION if ||u_final|| vanishes at working tolerance, otherwise OPEN_BOOST_WORD_ROTATION. This is Thomas's original product of successive Lorentz boosts [5]. Out-and-back paths do not invert, because the two lab boosts are not mutual inverses in a fixed frame.

**Chart.** The Palge–Pfeifer spatial connection in Cartesian velocity coordinates is the Thomas 1-form

```
ω = (γ² / (γ + 1)) β × dβ
```

Path-ordered with Richardson extrapolation, this evaluation is regular at rest and at the spherical poles. The same connection pulled back to spherical angles (θ, φ) is singular at the poles and at rest, which is exactly the BU+–BU− geodesic.

## 4. The Three-Axis Precession Triad

The elementary object is the triple of origin-pair gyrotriangle defects on the mutually orthogonal stage vectors.

| Pair | Defect | Axis of origin-gyr | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| origin–UNA–ONA | ω_UO = 0.396601502221 | +z | steering generator |
| origin–ONA–BU+ | ω_OB = 0.097671089129 | +x | balance generator; ω_corner |
| origin–BU+–UNA | ω_UB = 0.083413894169 | +y | coupling generator |

Each origin-gyration angle equals the corresponding Ungar defect to the working floor, and the three axes are mutually orthogonal. These are the Wigner rotations of composing the two origin boosts of each pair. A carried spatial frame rotates by that angle. They are not equal-speed Wigner evaluations.

The equal-speed calibration ω_0 = TW(u_p, u_p; θ = o_p) = 0.215549910153 is a different function of the thresholds. It is the Wigner angle of two boosts of common speed u_p separated by o_p, and it is not ω_UO. Analysis_Holonomy.md records the closed forms dω/dβ = (12√2 − 4)/17 and dω/dθ = (21 − 12√2)/17 at that point; their sum is 1 as an algebraic identity. Those derivatives partition an equal-speed response, not the stage-pair triad.

Composites of the triad are forced by the same geometry.

```
δ_BU       = 2 ω_OB = defect(ONA, BU+, BU−)     = 0.195342178258
```

The dual-pole identity δ_BU = 2 ω_OB is the closed form of Analysis_Holonomy.md written in triad language: the middle edge BU+–BU− is collinear, hence the identity gyration, and the two ONA–BU corners share one axis. The UNA dual-pole loop is a different polygon, not a transported copy of δ_BU. The three-stage defect δ_UOB is the noncommutative composite of the three elementary rotations. It is not their scalar sum.

## 9. Chart Connection

The Cartesian Thomas 1-form of Section 3 is regular at rest and at the spherical poles. Richardson extrapolation on n and 2n steps recovers δ_BU on both the BU loop and the palindrome to about 10⁻⁸. That evaluation belongs to the canonical aperture class.

The spherical (θ, φ) path-ordered exponential is a coordinate readout of the same connection. Stage coordinates in the z-chart place BU+ at θ = 0 and BU− at θ = π, both poles. The x-chart poles UNA; the y-chart poles ONA. A diagonal chart avoids stage poles and still fails to recover δ_BU (G_diag ≈ 1.051 on the BU loop). The z-chart offset is

```
G_z(BU)  = 0.0512616448
G_z(pal) = 0.0513934841
G_diag − G_z ≈ 0.9999524936
```

These G values are classified COORDINATE_READOUT. They are not a third holonomy, not a Dirac-string theorem, and not a discrepancy in the connection. Analysis_Holonomy.md recorded the spherical offset and left a complete chart evaluation outside the verified aperture class. The Cartesian construction closes that gap.

The BU loop is planar in the yz-plane. The palindrome spans all three axes. Planarity is a property of the vertex set, not of the chart.

## 12. Metric Ontology

Each reported number is a `MetricRecord` with five tags: origin layer, transport law, invariant type, physical status, and a downstream whitelist. The whitelist is the name set {m_a, δ_BU, ρ, Δ} (with secant aliases). It records what gravity already consumes. It is not a license to reprint τ_G or Mercury.

Status values used here:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FORCED | fixed by the thresholds under the stated connection, with no remaining choice |
| ELEMENTARY | an origin-pair generator of the canonical triad |
| COMPOSITE | a word in those generators, or an arithmetic combination of forced quantities |
| CALIBRATION | equal-speed Wigner response; not a stage-pair gyration |
| PROTOCOL | lab boost-composition residual |
| COORDINATE_READOUT | spherical-chart evaluation of the same connection |
| UNKNOWN | measured, with no named physical coupling |

Forced canonical quantities include the three priors that enter the ball, the three Einstein-speed identifications, δ_BU, the secant pair (ρ, Δ), the linear intercept ρ_0, the tangent pair (ρ_tangent, Δ_tangent), and the Cartesian Thomas Pexp. Elementary quantities are ω_UO, ω_OB, ω_UB, and the palindrome axis-transport angle. Composite quantities include δ_UNA_BU, δ_UOB, and the identity bin. Protocol quantities include every lab angle and every scalar F. Coordinate readouts include every G_sph. Unknown quantities are φ_SU2, the compact–hyperbolic residual, σ_compact, cyc4, and L4_crossed.

Universality in this analysis means the same transport geometry on the velocity manifold, with different connections and projections. It does not mean that every angle equals δ_BU.

## 13. What Is Not Claimed

Canonical bins are not Standard Model couplings, mixing angles, or dark-sector scales. The ratios F_pal / √3 and F_BU / π are not laws. Spin detects frame rotation; it does not generate it. A pair defect is not a precession rate. Equivalent circular speeds are not orbital speeds of CGM stages. The triad exhibits three orthogonal Wigner axes; it is not an independent proof that space is three-dimensional. The compact–hyperbolic residual is not a correction factor for α. This program does not evaluate kernel clocks, optical depth, or perihelion.

## 14. Falsification Criteria

The analysis fails if any of the following occurs.

1. Any of geodesic holonomy, origin-gyration, the dual-pole word, or the Ungar defect on (ONA, BU+, BU−) departs from δ_BU = 4 · arctan(k(π/4) · k(m_a)) beyond the working floor.
2. The Cartesian Thomas Pexp (Richardson) on the BU loop or the palindrome departs from δ_BU beyond 10⁻⁸.
3. Origin-gyration and geodesic holonomy disagree on any closed walk of length at most 5.
4. The canonical spectrum at L ≤ 5 contains an angle that is not one of the six bins of Section 5, or a directed stage edge that is not UO, OB, UB, or I.
5. The three origin-pair gyration axes fail to be mutually orthogonal, or any origin-gyration angle fails to equal the corresponding Ungar defect.
6. The palindrome fails R_pal = A⁻¹ R_BU A, or the axis-transport angle fails to equal ω_UO, or the BU axis fails to be perpendicular to the UNA–ONA axis.
7. A lab word other than a collinear BU± out-back closes at L ≤ 5, or a named BU or palindrome lab word is closed.
8. The shared constants δ_BU, ρ, and Δ disagree with `BU_HOLONOMY_ANGLE`, `RHO`, and `APERTURE_GAP` beyond 10⁻¹².
9. ρ_0 fails to equal both 2 k(o_p) and d(δ_BU)/d(m_a) at m_a = 0.

## 16. Conclusion

Placing the CGM stage thresholds as Einstein speeds produces a finite discrete spectrum of canonical holonomy angles. The spectrum is generated by three mutually orthogonal origin-pair Wigner rotations together with the identity on the collinear BU poles. The dual-pole loop angle δ_BU is twice the ONA–BU generator. The palindrome preserves that angle and transports the axis by the UNA–ONA generator. Linear, secant, and tangent readouts of the same closed form decompose the aperture into a baseline gap fixed by ONA, a finite-BU correction, and an operating-point differential. Laboratory boost composition is a different protocol, closed only on collinear pole exchange. The regular Cartesian Thomas 1-form belongs to the canonical class; spherical Pexp does not.

Gravity already consumes m_a, δ_BU, ρ, and Δ from this geometry. The remaining elementary angles, leftover generator words, and the compact–hyperbolic residual are measured and left without named couplings.
